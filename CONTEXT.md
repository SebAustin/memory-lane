# Memory Lane

Helps people who care for someone living with dementia run personalized reminiscence sessions, using cultural cues that people of the same era and place loved.

## People

**Person**:
The individual living with dementia whom the sessions are for.
_Avoid_: Patient, user, client, resident

**Caregiver**:
Whoever prepares and runs sessions with the Person: a family member, activity coordinator, music therapist or volunteer. The Caregiver is the app's user.
_Avoid_: User, staff, admin

**Visitor**:
Someone, often a grandchild, who joins a session and whose own favorites can be compared with the Person's.
_Avoid_: Guest, family member

## Profile

**Life Story**:
What the Caregiver knows about the Person's background: birth year, Hometown, Young-Adult City, heritage, occupation, Seeds, Avoid List and Dementia Stage.
_Avoid_: Profile, intake, patient record

**Hometown**:
Where the Person grew up. It is the location signal for cultural affinity.
_Avoid_: Birthplace, origin

**Care Location**:
Where the Person lives now. It anchors any suggested outings or local places.
_Avoid_: Address, facility

**Seed**:
A remembered favorite (an artist, film, dish, brand…) that has been confirmed as a specific Qloo entity.
_Avoid_: Favorite, input, interest

**Avoid List**:
Topics, people or works the Caregiver says must not appear, because they may cause distress.
_Avoid_: Blacklist, triggers, filters

**Dementia Stage**:
Early, middle or late. It shapes how a Session is formatted (conversation-led vs. sensory-led), not which cues are chosen.
_Avoid_: Severity, level

**Reminiscence Window**:
The span of years when the Person was roughly 10–30 years old. Memories from this "reminiscence bump" are the most vivid and lasting.
_Avoid_: Era, decade, nostalgia range

**Taste Profile**:
The current set of Seeds, Learned Favorites and Exclusions used to ask Qloo for cues. It evolves as Reactions are logged.
_Avoid_: Preferences, model, persona

**Learned Favorite**:
A Cue that drew an Engaged Reaction and has been promoted into the Taste Profile.
_Avoid_: Like, upvote

**Exclusion**:
An entity or tag the Taste Profile tells Qloo to leave out, either from the Avoid List or from a Distressed Reaction.
_Avoid_: Ban, block

## Sessions

**Cue**:
A single cultural item (an artist, a film, a TV show, a book, a place or dish, a brand) presented in a Session to spark memory and conversation. Every Cue is a Qloo entity; music Cues are artists, not individual songs.
_Avoid_: Recommendation, item, card, result

**Prompt**:
An open, failure-free conversation starter attached to a Cue (for example "Tell me about the dances you went to"). It never tests recall.
_Avoid_: Question, quiz

**Session**:
A themed, 20–45 minute sequence of Cues, Prompts and sensory activities that a Caregiver runs with the Person.
_Avoid_: Activity, lesson, playlist

**Kit**:
A week's worth of Sessions generated for one Person.
_Avoid_: Plan, pack, bundle

**Reaction**:
The Caregiver's observation of the Person's response to a Cue: Engaged, Neutral or Distressed.
_Avoid_: Rating, feedback, score

**Session Log**:
The record of Reactions from Sessions that have been run.
_Avoid_: History, journal

**Provenance**:
Why a Cue was chosen: which Seeds and signals (age cohort, Hometown, Reminiscence Window) drove it, and Qloo's affinity score.
_Avoid_: Explanation, reasoning, metadata

**Baseline Kit**:
A Kit produced by the LLM alone, without Qloo. It is shown only for comparison.
_Avoid_: Control, generic kit
